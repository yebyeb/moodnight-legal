MoodNight

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use MoodNight (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to this policy.

The short version: MoodNight has no user accounts, no servers of our own and no analytics. Every sound file ships inside the app and the music layers are generated on your device as they play, so listening works with no connection at all. We receive nothing about you.

Two things do reach a network, and neither of them is us. Buying, restoring or managing a subscription is a conversation with the store you installed the App from — Apple on iPhone, Google Play on Android. And the Android version shows ads, which are served by Google AdMob; that is the one part of the App that collects anything, and section 7 explains exactly what. The iPhone version has no ads at all, and a subscription removes the ads on Android.

1. What MoodNight Does

MoodNight plays and mixes nature soundscapes — rain, ocean, forest, crickets, and similar — so you can fall asleep, relax or focus. You can layer several sounds together, set their volumes, save the result as your own mix, and run a sleep timer that fades the audio out.

MoodNight also offers an optional paid subscription, which unlocks layering several sounds at once and the rest of the catalogue.

All audio is bundled with the App or synthesised on your device in real time: nothing is streamed or downloaded, and playback works in airplane mode. The App reaches the network for the subscription actions the store handles — reading the current prices, completing a purchase, and restoring an earlier one — and, on Android, to fetch the ads described in section 7. If there is no connection, listening is unaffected.

2. Information We Collect

We collect nothing. We do not receive, or have access to, any information about you or your use of the App. Specifically, the App has:

  • no account or login system;
  • no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, no social-media SDK;
  • no server of our own — the App never sends anything to us;
  • no access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or location.

We do not build user profiles, and we do not sell or share data, because we do not receive any.

One exception, on Android only: the Android version shows ads, and the advertising SDK that serves them does collect information — not for us, and we never see it. It is described in full in section 7. The iPhone version contains no advertising SDK, and subscribing on Android removes the ads.

3. What Is Stored on Your Device and in Your iCloud

The App saves a small amount of data locally, using the operating system's standard app-preferences storage, so that it can remember your setup between sessions:

  • your settings — chosen language, light/dark theme, sleep-timer duration, the star sign you picked if any, the transition interval, and the name you typed if you gave one;
  • your saved mixes — the names you gave them, the sounds they contain, and the volume of each layer;
  • your playlists — their names and which of your mixes they hold;
  • the mix that was playing last, so the App can restore it;
  • how many nights you have used the App, and when the system rating prompt was last requested, so that you are not asked again too soon;
  • whether you have completed the initial mood question, whether the introductory subscription screen has been shown once, and whether a subscription has ever been active on this device — the last of these only so that the App does not show a sales screen to someone who has already paid.

This data is a few kilobytes of text. It stays in the App’s private storage area on your device. We cannot read it, retrieve it, or recover it for you.

iCloud

So that your mixes, playlists and settings are not lost when you reinstall the App or switch devices, this same small set of data is also kept in your own iCloud account, using Apple’s built-in iCloud storage. It is your iCloud, under your Apple Account — we operate no server, receive no copy, and cannot read it. Nothing else is stored there: no audio, no listening history, no mood answers. If you are not signed in to iCloud, or turn iCloud off for MoodNight in your device settings, the App works the same and keeps the data on the device only.

On Android there is no iCloud copy: this same small set of data stays on the device only, in the App's private storage, and is removed when you uninstall the App.

4. The Mood Check-In

The App can ask how you are feeling and suggest a matching soundscape. Your answer is used immediately, on your device, to pick a sound — it is not stored as a history, not analysed, and not sent anywhere. Closing the App discards it.

5. Background Playback

So that the sound continues when your screen locks, the App publishes the current track title to the system's lock-screen player controls — using the background audio capability on iPhone, and a media-playback foreground service on Android. Both are handled entirely by the operating system on your device. No playback information is reported to us or to any third party, and nothing about what you listen to is used for advertising.

6. Purchases

MoodNight offers an optional auto-renewing subscription, sold as a monthly or a yearly plan. It is sold and processed entirely by the store you installed the App from — the Apple App Store using your Apple Account on iPhone, or Google Play using your Google account on Android. We never see or store your payment details, card numbers, or billing address, and we cannot tell who subscribed.

To know whether to unlock the paid features, the App asks the store on your device whether a valid subscription exists. That check is made by the store's own framework (StoreKit on iPhone, Google Play Billing on Android), the answer stays on your device, and it is never sent to us. Either store may provide us with anonymous, aggregated sales reports that cannot identify you. Their handling of purchase data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Third-Party Services and Advertising

On iPhone, the only third party involved in normal use is Apple — for app distribution, for the subscription described above, and for the occasional rating prompt, which is Apple's own system dialog. The iPhone version embeds no other third-party service, no analytics and no advertising.

On Android, there are two: Google Play, for app distribution and for the subscription, and Google AdMob, which serves the ads in the free version.

What the ads collect

We do not receive any of it. AdMob is Google's advertising service, and to choose and measure an ad it may process your device's advertising ID, your IP address, and general information about the device and the ad shown. This is governed by Google's privacy policy; a list of what Google collects for ads is published by Google and is not something we control or can see. We hold no copy and build no profile.

Where the ads appear

Only between listening sessions, and only when no sound is playing — never over audio that is already running, and never while a sleep timer is counting down.

Your choice

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's own consent form appears before any ad is requested, and you can allow or refuse personalised advertising. You can change that decision at any time from Settings → Ad privacy inside the App.
  • On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads, which also lets you opt out of ad personalisation system-wide.
  • A subscription removes the ads entirely, and with them the advertising SDK's requests.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We collect no personal information from anyone, so no such data can be gathered from children by us.

On Android, the ads described in section 7 are requested as not directed to children, which instructs Google to withhold personalised advertising and to apply the restrictions it uses for child-directed treatment. If a child uses the App, a subscription removes the ads entirely.

9. Data Retention & Deletion

Your data lives on your device and, if iCloud is on, in your own iCloud account. To remove a saved mix or playlist, delete it inside the App; the deletion carries to your other devices. To remove everything, uninstall the App and turn iCloud off for MoodNight in your device settings — uninstalling alone leaves the iCloud copy, which is what lets your mixes come back if you install the App again. We hold no server-side copies, so there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

10. Your Rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights to access, correct, delete and port your personal data. Because MoodNight does not collect or receive personal data, we hold no records about you to disclose, amend, or erase. You retain full control of your data directly on your device and, on iPhone, in your own iCloud account.

For the advertising described in section 7, the controller is Google, not us. You can withdraw or change your advertising consent at any time from Settings → Ad privacy inside the App, reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings, or remove the ads altogether by subscribing. Requests about data Google holds should be directed to Google using the links in section 7.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are effective immediately after being posted on this page, and the "Last Updated" date above will be revised. If we ever introduce an optional feature that sends data off your device, this policy will be updated before that feature ships, and the feature will be off unless you turn it on.
